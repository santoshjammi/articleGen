Country's News will pivot from:

> high-volume AI-generated commodity news

to:

# a focused intelligence platform analyzing how AI, automation, enterprise software, and smart infrastructure are reshaping industries.

The platform will specialize in:

* AI infrastructure
* enterprise transformation
* smart mobility / EV ecosystems
* India digital transformation
* operational intelligence
* strategic analysis

The system will combine:

* AI-assisted research
* structured intelligence extraction
* editorial scoring
* semantic topic clustering
* automated publishing
* analytics feedback loops
* monetization optimization

to create a compounding authority ecosystem.
